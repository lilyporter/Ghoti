\section{Introduction}

This document covers the hazard analysis for the Ghoti project, a web-based software assistant for speech-language pathologist (SLPs) 
during sessions. The system captures and analyses a child’s speech in real-time, and flags it for substitution errors. Results are 
presented to the SLP through a dashboard interface and data visualizations. The system also offers in-session exercises to support 
the SLP in coaching a patient on speech challenges. It is strictly assistive and does not replace the professional judgement of the 
SLP. 
This document defines hazard as a property or condition in the system together with a condition in the environment that has the 
potential to cause harm or loss. 
The purpose of this document is to identify the hazards of the system, so mitigation strategies and safety requirements can be 
derived. 

\section{Scope and Purpose of Hazard Analysis}

This hazard analysis addresses the major hazards that could negatively impact the Ghoti project. This includes hazards with 
speech/audio capture, speech analysis, and interfaces. The analysis will cover the various risks such as with patient data handling, 
data privacy, accuracy, and performance. The losses that could result from the hazards include violations of PHIPA, privacy breaches, inaccurate analysis of speech and presentation of data, and loss of stakeholder trust.
The purpose of this hazard analysis is early identification of any risks that could affect the key components of the system. 
Identifying these risks will inform the creation of mitigation strategies and proper safety and security requirements. 
Doing this will minimize risk and potential loss from unaddressed hazards.
